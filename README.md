## 📄 Resume Writing (10 prompts)

1. "Act as a professional resume writer. Rewrite my bullet points below to use strong action verbs and quantify impact where possible: [paste bullets]."
2. "I'm applying for a [job title] role. Based on this job description: [paste JD], rewrite my resume summary to match the keywords and tone."
3. "Turn this list of my responsibilities into 3-4 achievement-focused bullet points using the STAR method: [paste responsibilities]."
4. "Review my resume for ATS compatibility. Flag any formatting, keyword, or structure issues: [paste resume text]."
5. "I'm a [your field] student with no full-time experience. Help me write a resume summary that highlights projects, coursework, and internships."
6. "Shorten this resume bullet to one line under 20 words without losing impact: [paste bullet]."
7. "Suggest 10 strong action verbs to replace 'responsible for' and 'worked on' in a resume."
8. "Create a skills section for a [role] resume, grouped into Technical Skills, Tools, and Soft Skills."
9. "Compare my resume against this job posting and tell me what's missing: [paste both]."
10. "Write a one-line resume headline/title for a [your role] that's specific, not generic."

## 🎤 Interview Preparation (10 prompts)

3. "Using the STAR method, help me structure an answer about a time I [situation, e.g., resolved a conflict]."
